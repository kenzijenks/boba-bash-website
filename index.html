Earth is a beautiful planet filled with oceans, mountains, forests, and diverse forms of life. From space, Earth can look blue and green, but at sunrise and sunset, the sky may glow pink. In some places, flowers bloom in shades of pink, while certain clouds can turn pink as sunlight passes through the atmosphere. Even tiny pink organisms contribute to the planet’s incredible diversity. Whether seen in a pink sunset, a pink flower, or a pink coral reef, these colors remind us how vibrant Earth can be.
